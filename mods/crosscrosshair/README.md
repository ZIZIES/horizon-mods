# crosscrosshair for Horizon

A crosshair you draw yourself, in the way of the Custom Crosshair Mod's draw
mode on Java. Written for Horizon, not ported from it.

It starts as a small cross: the middle pixel, one on each side of it, and one
more under the bottom one.

Options > Mods > crosscrosshair has a 15x15 grid (the size of vanilla's
crosshair) under the buttons. Left click or drag draws, right click or drag
erases; the reddish cell is the middle of the screen. Beside it is the
crosshair at its real size.

- **Colour**: white, inverted (as vanilla's), black, red, green, blue,
  yellow, aqua, pink.
- **Outline**: a black edge around the pixels.
- **Reset to the cross** and **Clear**.

The attack indicator under the crosshair stays. Horizon's own custom crosshair
(Extras > PvP) draws as well when it is on, so turn that one off.

Not here: more than one colour in a crosshair, sizes other than 15x15, and a
grid on windows too short for one (under about 190 GUI pixels high).

Status: attempted. Compiles into the game; not played yet.
