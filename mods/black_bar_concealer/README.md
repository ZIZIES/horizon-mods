# Black Bar Concealer for Horizon

After Gerrygames' Fabric mod (CC0). A skin drawn for slim (3 pixel) arms but
set to the wide model shows black bars down its arms. The mod spots such skins
when they download (both arms leave the wide-only pixels transparent) and:

- by default wears them on the **slim model**, first-person arm and 3D skin
  layers included;
- with **Prefer Wide Model** (Options > Mods) keeps the wide arms and stretches
  each 3 pixel face to 4 instead.

Limits: only other players and yourself on a server connection (single player
included), not mannequins or heads. A skin widened once stays widened until it
is downloaded again; switching the setting applies to skins loaded after it.

Status: attempted. Compiles into the game; not played yet.
