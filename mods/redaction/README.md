# REDACTION for Horizon

After Polyfrost's Fabric mod (GPL-3.0-or-later), written from what it does.
Everything is off until turned on in Options > Mods > REDACTION, as in the
original.

- **Blackbar**: the hotbar becomes a flat bar across the whole screen, with a
  highlight that slides between slots. Opacity (33/50/75/100%), highlight
  strength and slot numbers are settings.
- **Custom Hand FOV**: the hand and held item get a field of view of their own
  (30 to 150, default 125).
- **Last Server Joined Button**: a button in the title screen's top left
  corner that rejoins the last server you connected to.
- **Inventory Snow**: snow around the inventory and creative screens (50 to
  300 flakes).

Not here: the server preview in Direct Connect, free colour pickers, lines
between flakes near the cursor, and Blackbar on the spectator menu.

Status: attempted. Compiles into the game; not played yet.
