# Tree Feller for Horizon

Sneak and break one log of a tree with an axe: the rest of its logs and all its leaves
break too, and a sapling is planted where the trunk stood.

- Logs break as you break them, so the held tool wears once a log (and its
  enchantments count). If the tool breaks on the way, the felling stops there.
- Leaves break by hand, so they don't wear the tool; they drop what leaves
  drop (saplings, sticks, apples by chance).
- Drops go to your inventory; what doesn't fit drops at your feet. The log you
  broke yourself drops as usual.
- Replanting takes one sapling per trunk block that stood on soil (4 for a
  2x2 tree) out of what the leaves dropped, else out of your inventory. No
  sapling, no replant. Free in creative.
- Only grown trees: logs with natural leaves on them. Log builds, logs with
  only placed leaves, and anything over 512 logs are left alone. Nether fungi
  aren't felled (no leaves). Mangroves fell but rarely replant (the trunk
  stands on roots, not soil).
- Works where your game runs the world (singleplayer, a LAN world you host).
  On someone else's server it does nothing.

No settings; turn it off in Options > Mods.

Status: attempted. Compiles into the game and its tree-finding is unit
tested. Played once: felling works, replanting did not; reworked since, not replayed.
